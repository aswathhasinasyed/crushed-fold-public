Quick Troubleshooting

  • No Haptics or Sound: Ensure your iPhone is not in Low Power Mode and that Silent Mode / system haptics are enabled in iOS Settings > Sounds & Haptics.
  • Dual-Device Pairing Issues: Make sure Bluetooth and Local Network permissions are enabled for CRUSHED in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network.
  • Restore Purchases: Open the Specimen Deck settings and tap Restore Purchases using the same Apple ID.
