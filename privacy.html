Privacy Policy

App: CRUSHED • Effective Date: November 15, 2026

CRUSHED is designed with privacy as a core principle. All physics simulations, haptics, sound effects, and device interactions run 100% locally on your iPhone.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data, usage analytics, device identifiers, or crash logs.

2. Device Sensors & Local Network

  • Motion & Hinge Sensors: Accelerometer, gyroscope, and fold-angle telemetry are processed strictly in real time on your device to drive squish physics and tilt-based fluid animations. Sensor data is never recorded or transmitted.
  • Local Network (Peer-to-Peer): If you link two iPhones together for dual-screen play, the connection occurs directly over your local Wi-Fi or Bluetooth peer-to-peer session. No data passes through external servers.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional object packs and upgrades are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit. We never receive or store your payment information.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because we collect zero personal information from any user, CRUSHED complies with COPPA and global children’s privacy standards.
